# Actual live coverage, 8 October 2026

This audit covers the accepted live corpus only: the original direct records, owner-uploaded unverified earthquake claims and the first reviewed snapshot batch. Pending EU Council and TWSE additions, country-source candidates, and manual major-company/unicorn evidence are excluded. The JSON audit binds the exact readback and frozen fixture digests. Record/index totals establish processing scope; they do not establish information coverage.

## Time

Source collection occurred on 7–8 October. The reviewed snapshots were fetched around 09:29–09:49 UTC on 8 October and imported into the Site around 10:42–11:07 UTC. Source publication, event occurrence, first geometry observation, statistical period, forecast validity and Site import/index clocks remain different fields.

The USGS request was exactly `summary/2.5_week.geojson`: a rolling seven-day magnitude 2.5+ feed. The stored direct selection covers earthquake times from **5 October 20:22 UTC to 7 October 23:35 UTC**, because only the newest 100 of 314 upstream entries were retained. The uploaded snapshot extends claims to 8 October 03:00 UTC; it remains unverified and overlaps most direct IDs. This is not all earthquakes or a complete seven-day archive.

The selected GDACS bulletin publication span is **2–7 October**, while source event fields extend back to 19 September. A refreshed bulletin is not a new disaster. EONET includes approximate first-geometry dates as early as **28 May 2021**; open or recently updated geometry can belong to an old event. It does not establish precise onset or a complete recent-event timeline.

Only 217 live record units have publication fields. Sixty are BIS midnight dates whose exact release time is unverified. Of the remaining 157 source-timestamped publication records, **43 fall within 24 hours and 146 within seven days at the frozen reference time 8 October 11:49:16 UTC**. The 24-hour group is 34 GDACS bulletins, eight UN-selected Chinese headlines and one NASA notice. These windows must not be presented as current at a later time or as news completeness. Most reference/statistical records have no applicable publication/event clock.

Eurostat size/sector observations are **2022–2024**; annual FX is **2019–2025**. Morocco release headlines discuss 2026 Q2/August and a 2014 census quality report. Three SEC profiles contain filing dates from **11 September–5 October**, with acceptance times through 6 October. The contents are mostly Forms 4/144, not operating results or incidents.

Malaysia fuel dates are **effective dates, 1 and 8 October**, not publication timestamps. Sarawak's **8–14 October** dates are forward forecast validity. Its issuance/publication time is unknown. Three weather heading records contain timezone-naive issue/validity fields, so verified absolute freshness and current applicability are unknown.

Daily autonomous operation is not active. The prior hosted automation is paused; the local routine requires an open, awake process, and unattended Windows operation/automatic sync are unverified.

## Geography

The **250-area directory is identity coverage**: 248 canonical UNSD statistical areas and two separately labeled FAQ exceptions, with six source-provided language names. It supplies no current events and makes no sovereignty assertion.

Actual quantitative business context covers **36 European reporting geographies plus an EU aggregate**, and annual FX covers **16 named economies**. These are dated statistical locations. They are not company operating footprints or current impact areas.

EONET supplies points or bounded geometry samples; seven extents are truncated and cannot establish full affected areas. Its source/title geography must not be replaced with the NASA publisher's US jurisdiction.

Malaysia fuel is national context. The warning headings do not retain a verified affected district/facility. Sarawak is one state-wide forecast area with no coordinates; it does not prove local flooding or disruption.

GLEIF's sample has **46 legal-country and 46 headquarters-country labels**. Those are separate identity fields and do not establish operating or impact locations. CVM PAIS is retained as registry-country/raw; headquarters meaning is unknown. SEC registry and TWSE exchange jurisdiction also do not identify headquarters. No common time/domain/geography denominator supports a global completeness percentage.

Recurring company disclosure/incident tracking is not operational. Major-company size and current unicorn valuation evidence are not live.

Political aggregate data is limited to source-defined groups/parties in **Japan, South Africa and Canada**. Japan is dated 24 September; the 23 Canadian/South African aggregates have unknown effective dates. Eleven groups meet a declared 5% chamber-seat criterion; that does not establish worldwide political organization coverage, governing status or ideology.

The uploaded 100 earthquake claims overlap **94 direct USGS IDs**, so they add six claimed IDs, not 100 independent earthquakes or a second confirming source. EONET also contains GDACS references, so feed/report totals must not become independent corroboration counts.

## Domains and useful limits

The strongest actual signals are earthquake facts; GDACS/EONET wildfire, flood, storm and ice metadata; Malaysian fuel/weather; ECB/BIS monetary/banking material; UN-selected Chinese affairs headlines; NASA technology notices; issuer/legal identity references; and dated enterprise-size/sector aggregates.

Eurostat's admitted sector selection is manufacturing, wholesale/retail and information/communication. It is not worldwide SME coverage or a legal SME membership classification. Employment strata overlap, and omitted/suppressed cells remain unknown. CVM/TWSE industry labels are source schemes, not a harmonized global industry map.

The main gaps are broad recent business/general news; every-country event timelines; firm/supplier/facility incidents and operating footprints; recurring major-company disclosures; current complete unicorn evidence; worldwide SME trends and political organizations; sanctions/trade/customs/regulatory completeness; independent corroboration; and complete historical archives.

Cited lexical retrieval is usable for this bounded corpus. Collection and index completion remain different from geographic, temporal, entity and domain coverage completion. Source-specific licenses/use restrictions accompany downstream exports; the audit does not enlarge reuse permission or add customer analysis.
