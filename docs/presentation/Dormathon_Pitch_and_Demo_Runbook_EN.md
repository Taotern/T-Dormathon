## Study and source notes

### External forecasting protocol

UCI Online Retail II describes a UK non-store retailer in 2009–2011. The target is positive recorded item transaction sales in units, used as a demand proxy. Returns, cancellations and invalid or non-product rows follow frozen exclusion rules. A complete-ledger assumption interprets absent interior SKU dates as zero recorded sales; this does not establish zero latent demand. All 135 whole-ledger transaction-free dates remain disclosed.

Training: 1 December 2009–13 October 2011. Validation: 14 October–10 November 2011. Primary test: 11 November–8 December 2011. The final transaction date is excluded as incomplete. Training-only eligibility retains 3,231 known SKUs. Twenty-two daily origins and seven horizons produce 497,574 forecast tasks, versus 90,468 unique item-days.

The study assumes retrospective transaction-time availability. Historical acquisition logs, stockouts, real inventory, supplier lead times and delivery outcomes are unavailable. It establishes neither cold-start performance nor cross-company generalization. Source rows never enter the MR D.I.Y. operational namespace.

### Metric detail for a technical question

Test MAE, units: weekly naive 7.727; retained 28-day mean 6.974; direct ridge 7.607. Retained-mean test WAPE: 103.41%. WAPE is total absolute error divided by actual units over the forecast tasks. Ridge-minus-mean WAPE is +9.38 percentage points, with a descriptive bootstrap interval of +7.05 to +12.65 points. Four short time blocks limit inference. No direct mean-minus-weekly-naive confidence interval was supplied, so no significance claim is made for that pair.
